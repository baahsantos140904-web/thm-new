The content focused on using Linux through the command line, exploring essential commands for managing files, directories, and navigating the system's structure.

Commands such as ls, cd, pwd, and find were used to locate files and explore directories. I also practiced using touch, mkdir, cp, mv, and rm to create, organize, copy, move, and delete files and folders.

Another important topic was understanding the Linux filesystem structure, identifying directories such as /etc, /var, /root, and /tmp, as well as learning their respective purposes.
The practical exercises required attention to commands and the ability to interpret terminal errors, helping me become more independent when performing tasks.

These skills are essential for working with Linux environments and developing a strong foundation in system administration and Cybersecurity.
